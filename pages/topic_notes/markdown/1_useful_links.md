## [Falstad.com](https://falstad.com)

- Browser based circuit simulation
- Great for understanding current flow, filters, oscillators and trasnsient behaviour
- Looks in between tinkerkad and wokwi

## [Digikey.com/en/schemeit/](https://www.digikey.com/en/schemeit)

- Quickly drawing and sharing circuit diagrams wihtout opening full PCB-design program

## [MIT opencourseware](https://ocw.mit.edu)

- Free uni-level courses, lecture notes, assignments, videos for circuits, signlas, electronics, electromagnetics
- MIT’s 6.002 course is specifiacally designed as introductory undergrad EE course
