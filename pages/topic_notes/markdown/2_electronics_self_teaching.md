  - How to use Ohm's Law
    - Can be rearranged depending on which part of equation you know

    - To find current (I) :
      - I= V/R

    - To find resistance (R):
      - R = V/I

    - To find voltage (V):
      - V=IxR

    - As long as you know 2 values, you can get the third so you don’t mess with real components needlessly
