  - What is ohms law
    - describes relationship between voltrage current and resistance
    - Crucial for calibration, analyzing and troubleshooting electircal circuits

    - E = I x R
    - Voltage = current x resistance
    - Volts = amps x ohms

  - Why was geoge ohm’s experiment important
    - Showed that electric current through conductor DIRECTLY PROPORTIONAL to voltage and INVERSELY PROPORTIONAL to resistance.
  - Key electrical elements explained
    | quantity | Ohm's Law Symbol | Unit of measure (abbrev) | role in circuits | in case you're wondering |
    | ---------- | ---------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
    | Voltage | E | Volt(V) | Pressure that triggers electron flow | E = electromotive force (old school term) |
    | Current | I | Ampere, Amp (A) | Rate of Electron flow | I = intensity |
    | Resistance | R | Ohm( Ω ) | Flow inhibitor | Ω = Greek letter omega |

  - How to use Ohm's Law
    - Can be rearranged depending on which part of equation you know

    - To find current (I) :
      - I= V/R

    - To find resistance (R):
      - R = V/I

    - To find voltage (V):
      - V=IxR

    - As long as you know 2 values, you can get the third so you don’t mess with real components needlessly
